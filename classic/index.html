Interactive desk

Drag a page and click it to read it, or use the menu above if you would rather jump to a section. The lamp switches light and dark. Drop an item in the dustbin to set it aside, or shake the bin to clear the desk and shake it again to restore everything.

Move mode is on. Drag items with your finger, then turn Move items off to scroll the page normally.
